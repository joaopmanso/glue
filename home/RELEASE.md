GLUE Home puts an icon next to the clock. While it runs, your other computers can send songs to this one from the GLUE website: they land in its incoming folder.

- **Windows:** `GLUE-Home-Setup.exe`. It isn't code-signed yet, so the browser may say it "isn't commonly downloaded" (⋯ › **Keep** › **Show more** › **Keep anyway**), and Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen (**More info** › **Run anyway**). Both are expected for a new app from an unknown publisher.
- **macOS:** `GLUE-Home.dmg`, for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. Drag GLUE Home to Applications. It isn't notarised yet: the first time, right-click (Control-click) it in Applications and choose **Open**.

Then connect it with a code from the GLUE website on the same computer (sidebar › Devices › + GLUE Home). It finds that computer's GLUE library and music folders by itself, and updates itself from then on.
