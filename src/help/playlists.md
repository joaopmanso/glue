## Playlists and folders
**+ Playlist** and **+ Folder** make them. Drag songs onto a playlist, drag playlists into folders, right-click for colours and more. A deleted playlist goes to **Recently deleted** first.

## Tags
Tag songs from the Tags column, or drag songs onto a tag in the sidebar. A tag is a quick list of its own.

To filter by tags, use the Tags column's header (or Filter): each tag you tick narrows the songs (the songs with all of them). Right-click tags in the sidebar › **Show only it here** to add tags instead (the songs with any of them).

## Building a playlist
**Build playlist from this** (with one or more songs selected) or **+ Auto** opens the builder. GLUE picks songs from your collection and puts them in an order that flows.
- **Flow:** choose a shape (Steady, Warm-up, Build to a peak, Peak and down, Peaks and breathers, Cool-down) and drag the curve's points to bend it. GLUE follows it with each song's **energy**, 1–10: how loud, busy and bright a song is next to the rest of your collection (Mixed In Key's "Energy N" in a comment counts as it is). Tick **Tempo follows the flow** to have the BPM rise and fall with it.
- **The songs you start from** aren't always first: each goes where it fits the flow, or where you say (**Opener**, **At the peak**, **Closer**).
- **Sound:** from varied to close to the songs you start from, and to each other. GLUE compares each song's sound (its spectrum's shape, brightness and low end), measured when it's analysed.
- Tempo, harmonic mixing, tags, ratings and songs to avoid work as before; the same artist is kept 3 songs apart unless you untick it.
- Each song in the result shows its energy (E1–E10) and how alike it sounds to the one before; the curve above shows the songs' energy against the flow. ↻ replaces one song, × removes it, **↻ Another** makes a new set.

Songs analysed before November 2026 are analysed once more for their sound profile; until then they count as average energy.

## Insights
**Insights** on an open playlist shows its length, tempos, keys and tags.

## Taking playlists out
Drag a playlist out of GLUE as an M3U8 file, or drag songs into your DJ app. With GLUE Home, the drag dock carries songs and playlists into Engine DJ or rekordbox.

## In Engine DJ
With GLUE Home running, right-click one of your playlists or folders › **Keep in Engine DJ**: it shows up in Engine DJ's
**GLUE** folder the next time Engine DJ starts, and so do your changes (how: [DJ libraries](#/help/dj-libraries)). The
Engine DJ icon shows by it (fainter for what's kept with its folder); **Stop keeping it in Engine DJ** takes it out of
Engine DJ; it stays here.

### Before November 2026: kept in step both ways (paused)
GLUE doesn't write into Engine DJ any more (see [DJ libraries](#/help/dj-libraries)). This is how it worked:

With your main Engine DJ library kept in step (see [DJ libraries](#/help/dj-libraries)), right-click one of your playlists or folders › **Keep in Engine DJ**. It goes into a **GLUE** folder in Engine DJ, inside the same folders as here, and the Engine DJ icon shows by it (fainter for what's kept with its folder).
- Both ways: rename it, move it, add, remove or reorder songs here or in Engine DJ, and the other follows. A playlist you make in Engine DJ's GLUE folder comes into GLUE.
- **Stop keeping it in Engine DJ** takes it out of the GLUE folder; it stays here.
- A playlist deleted on one side is asked about in the sidebar, under the library.
- GLUE Home writes into Engine DJ only while it's closed, after a backup.
- A song Engine DJ doesn't have is added to its collection, on its drive's Engine DJ library. One that can't be (its file isn't on this computer, or its drive has no Engine DJ library) stays in GLUE's playlist: a **!** by the Engine DJ icon, and the playlist says why.
- Songs in **TO BE SORTED** are in GLUE Home's incoming folder: **Move to music folder** (right-click) puts them in one of this collection's music folders.
