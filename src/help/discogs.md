GLUE Home can read your Discogs collection and wantlist, and look covers up on Discogs. It needs a personal access token from your Discogs account, and it only reads: nothing is changed on Discogs.

## Connecting Discogs
1. On discogs.com, open **Settings › Developers** and generate a personal access token.
2. In GLUE Home's window, under **Covers from Discogs**, paste it.
3. GLUE Home checks it with Discogs and says **Connected as** your Discogs name.

The token stays on the computer with GLUE Home and goes only to Discogs.

## Your records
**Discogs** in the sidebar (under Music) lists your **Collection** and your **Wantlist**: each record's cover, title, artists, label and catalogue number, year, styles and formats (Vinyl, 12", LP…).
- **In GLUE: 3 of 4 tracks** means GLUE found songs for 3 of the record's 4 tracks: the same title and version, by the track's artist, about as long. Click it to see them. Until GLUE Home has read a record's tracklist, it matches by the record's title as your songs' album (**In GLUE: 7 songs**).
- **Not in GLUE** means none were found: a record you have only on vinyl, or with different names here.
- **Choose…** on a record shows its tracks and the songs found for each. **Add** songs GLUE missed (search your library), or **Not on it** for one that isn't. GLUE remembers your choice.
- Show **All**, **In GLUE** or **Not in GLUE**, search by name, label or catalogue number, and sort by when you added it, artist, title, label or year.
- Right-click a record: **Show its songs**, **Play**, or **Open on Discogs**.

GLUE Home reads your lists again once a day, and on **Refresh**. A big collection takes a minute or two; then each new record's cover and tracklist, about a second each (Discogs allows about one request a second).

## Playlists
Make playlists of your records' tracks, mixed with your songs if you like, under **Discogs** in the sidebar.
- **+ Playlist** (in the Discogs view) makes one. Add a record (right-click › **Add to Discogs playlist**: its tracks, the ones you have here as your songs, the rest on vinyl), one track (the ＋ next to it in **Choose…**), or songs from your library (right-click › **Add to Discogs playlist**).
- Your songs play (▶, or **Play the digital ones**). A vinyl track shows its record, side and position, label and catalogue number, and **On vinyl**: the record to pull.
- Move tracks (right-click › **Move up**/**Move down**, or drag them), or remove them.
- **Build one from records or tracks:** tick records in the Discogs view and click **Build a playlist from these** (or right-click a record › **Build a playlist from this record**; in a Discogs playlist, tick tracks › **Build from these**). With nothing ticked, **+ Auto** builds from the rules alone.
  - Choose how many tracks, **tracks per record** (1, 2 or any), **Vinyl only / Digital only / Both**, whether to include compilations, add songs from your library, or avoid tracks already in your Discogs playlists.
  - Refine by styles and artists (**Prefer** or **Only these**), labels, formats and years.
  - GLUE picks tracks like the ones you started from: the same styles first, then genre, label, years and artists. **Surprise me** mixes it up.
  - **↻** swaps one track, **×** takes it out, **↻ Another** builds again. **Save** makes it a Discogs playlist you arrange by hand.

Discogs playlists stay under Discogs: they aren't among your library's playlists, and they don't go to Engine DJ.

## Covers
Songs whose tags have no cover get one looked up by GLUE Home. With Discogs connected, Discogs is asked too when the other services have none: often the only source for vinyl and white labels.
