GLUE Home can read your Discogs collection and wantlist, and look covers up on Discogs. It needs a personal access token from your Discogs account, and it only reads: nothing is changed on Discogs.

## Connecting Discogs
1. On discogs.com, open **Settings › Developers** and generate a personal access token.
2. In GLUE Home's window, under **Covers from Discogs**, paste it.
3. GLUE Home checks it with Discogs and says **Connected as** your Discogs name.

The token stays on the computer with GLUE Home and goes only to Discogs.

## Your records
**Discogs** in the sidebar (under Music) lists your **Collection** and your **Wantlist**: each record's cover, title, artists, label and catalogue number, year, styles and formats (Vinyl, 12", LP…).
- **In GLUE: 7 songs** means GLUE has songs of that record: the record's title as their album, by one of its artists (a compilation: on its label). Click it to see them.
- **Not in GLUE** means none were found: a record you have only on vinyl, or with different names here.
- Show **All**, **In GLUE** or **Not in GLUE**, search by name, label or catalogue number, and sort by when you added it, artist, title, label or year.
- Right-click a record: **Show its songs**, **Play**, or **Open on Discogs**.

GLUE Home reads your lists again once a day, and on **Refresh**. A big collection takes a minute or two, and its covers a little longer (Discogs allows about one request a second).

## Covers
Songs whose tags have no cover get one looked up by GLUE Home. With Discogs connected, Discogs is asked too when the other services have none: often the only source for vinyl and white labels.
