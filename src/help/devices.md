## An account (optional)
Sign in (Google, or email and password) to use your library on your other devices. GLUE Cloud keeps your library's data in step, never your music: songs play from the computer that has them, through GLUE Home.

## Collections
Your collections are your account's: every device sees them. Each computer adds its own music folders. A song on two computers (sent with GLUE Home, or copied) is one song: the song's row shows both computers, and each plays its own copy.

## When two devices change the same thing
If the same song (or playlist) was changed differently on two devices before they synced, GLUE keeps the account's version and asks you in a box: keep this device's, take the other's, or merge both (for tags and lists). With GLUE Home running, the box shows here all the same, and your answer is applied by GLUE Home. Changes made on the same computer (in the browser and by GLUE Home) never ask.

## Profiles
Profiles are your artist names, the same on every device. Each one opens the same library; pick one in **Who's using GLUE?** (click your name at the top).

## This computer
**Who's using GLUE?** also says how this computer uses GLUE (just here, synced, or with GLUE Home) and lets you change it.
