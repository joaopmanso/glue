- **Backup** (in "Who's using GLUE?") downloads your library as a .zip: playlists, ratings, notes, cues, everything but the music.
- GLUE keeps daily backups in your GLUE folder (`backups`), and one before anything that removes a lot.
- **Restore a backup** is offered when you set GLUE up.
- A deleted playlist goes to **Recently deleted** first.
