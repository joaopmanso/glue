## Music folders
**+ Folder** (under Music in the sidebar) adds a folder of music. GLUE keeps it up to date: new files come in, and songs whose files are gone show as missing. **Scan again** (the ↻ next to a folder) looks right away.

You can also **drop a folder** anywhere on the library. A folder inside one you've added already isn't added twice: its songs are there.

## Single songs
**+ Songs** adds files on their own, or drop them onto the page. With GLUE Home running, GLUE finds where a dropped song is: one in a music folder becomes that folder's song.

## Folders on a network or a drive
A music folder on a NAS, a network share or a drive that isn't always there is fine. While it can't be reached its songs wait: nothing is marked missing or changed, and GLUE carries on when it's back.

With GLUE Home running, GLUE Home reads your music folders, so it needs to know where a dropped folder is: it looks for a few seconds, and if it can't find it (a network folder usually isn't on any drive), its window asks you to choose it. GLUE says so meanwhile; the window may be behind your browser. A song or folder inside that can't be read is skipped, and the scan says how many.

## TO BE SORTED
Songs sent to this computer from your other devices land in **TO BE SORTED**. Move them into a music folder from there.
