GLUE is a music library for DJs that runs in your browser. It brings your music folders and your DJ apps' libraries (rekordbox, Engine DJ, Serato, Traktor, Apple Music) together, checks the real quality of every file, finds duplicates by sound, and helps you prepare sets. Your music never leaves your computers.

## What you need
- **Chrome or Edge on a computer.** GLUE reads your music folders through the browser; nothing is uploaded.
- **A place for GLUE's data.** GLUE asks for a folder (your "GLUE folder") where it keeps your library as plain files. You can back it up, sync it or move it like any folder.
- **Optional: an account,** to use your library on your laptop and phone.
- **Optional: GLUE Home,** a small app for your computer that analyses with the browser closed and plays your songs on your other devices. See [GLUE Home](#/help/glue-home).

## Your first five minutes
1. Choose where GLUE keeps its data, and make a profile (your name or DJ name).
2. Add your music folder. GLUE reads the tags at once, then listens to every song in the background.
3. If you use a DJ app, import its library: its playlists come in too.
4. Look at **Lower quality** and **Duplicates** in the sidebar: that's what GLUE found.
5. Double-click a song to see its page.

Gluey's button, at the bottom right of the library, has a tour of each feature.
