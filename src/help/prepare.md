The **Prepare** tab of a song's page is for getting it ready to mix.

## The beat grid
- GLUE sets the BPM and the first beat; check them with the **metronome** (M).
- **÷2** and **×2** fix half or double tempo; **Tap** (T) taps it along.
- **◀ ▶** move the grid earlier or later (shift for finer steps).
- Each profile can show BPMs as detected, folded into 60–120 or into 120–240 ("Who's using GLUE?").

## Cues and loops
Set cue points and loops on the waveform; they show in the overview above.

## Keys
Space plays and pauses; the arrows move along the song.
