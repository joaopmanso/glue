Open GLUE on your phone and sign in with the same account. Your library, playlists and tags are there; songs play from your computer through GLUE Home (it has to be running).

On the phone you can browse, search, play, rate, and build or edit playlists.

A song from your computer starts playing as soon as its first part arrives. If one is slow to start, open the player: the line under the artist says where the time went (connecting to your computer, the first answer, the first music) and how the phone reaches it: directly on the same network, through your router, or through GLUE Cloud's relay when the two can't reach each other (often on mobile data). Coming back to GLUE after a while in the background, the phone connects to your computer again straight away.
