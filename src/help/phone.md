Open GLUE on your phone and sign in with the same account. Your library, playlists and tags are there; songs play from your computer through GLUE Home (it has to be running).

On the phone you can browse, search, play, rate, and build or edit playlists.

A song from your computer starts playing as soon as its first part arrives. Coming back to GLUE after a while in the background, the phone connects to your computer again straight away.
