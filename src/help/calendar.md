The **Calendar** (at the top) keeps your gigs and sessions.

- **+ New event**: a date, a place, notes and a flyer.
- Give an event a **playlist**, or a version of one made for it.
- An event coming up with no music yet shows a badge on Calendar; GLUE Home reminds you too.
