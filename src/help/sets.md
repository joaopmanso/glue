## Mixing a playlist as a set
Right-click a playlist › **Mix it as a set…** GLUE lays its songs out as a mix, in the playlist's order: each song comes in on its first downbeat, on the beat of the one playing, on a 16-bar phrase, before its outro (from each song's sound profile).

- **Style:** **One at a time** (an 8-bar blend), **Continuous** (32-bar blends with a bass swap, shorter for short songs), **Double drops** (the next song's first drop lands on one of the playing song's drops, when their tempos are within 6 % and their keys fit; otherwise a bass swap). Songs whose tempos are more than 8 % apart are cut on the phrase instead.
- **Decks:** 2 or 3 lanes; the songs take turns.
- **Tempo:** each song's own (held through a hand-over, eased between), or **One tempo** for the whole set.
- **Key lock:** songs sped up or slowed down keep their key (on by default).
- Songs are levelled to the set's middle loudness, and where two play at full together (a double drop) both come down a little, as you'd ride the faders. Songs need their analysis (a tempo, and a beat grid from your DJ app, Prepare, or GLUE's own placement, marked "grid guessed").

## The timeline
Each song is drawn where it plays, as you'll hear it: its waveform gets smaller as it fades and loses its lows while its bass is cut, over a faint copy of the song as it is.

- **Drag a song** to bring it in earlier or later: the songs after it move with it, and the one before plays on as it was, so their hand-over gets longer or shorter.
- **Drag a song's left edge** to start it later or earlier in the song; **its right edge** to end it later or earlier.
- Everything moves a bar at a time, and only as far as the set still holds together: no silence between songs, no song cut short of a bar.
- **Fit**, **−** and **+** (or Ctrl + mouse wheel) zoom.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) step back through every change.

## Hand-overs
Each hand-over has a chip under the lanes (and a line in the running order). Click it to choose how it hands over (**Blend, Bass swap, Filter sweep, Echo out, Cut, Double drop**), make it 4 bars longer or shorter, bring the next song in earlier or later, or **Hear it**: it plays from 8 bars before.

## Listening
**Play** (or Space) plays the mix from the playhead, through your chosen output; click the ruler or a lane to move the playhead, or a song's time in the running order to play from there. The preview is the same mixer as the exported file, so what you hear is what you get. Songs are loaded as the mix reaches them ("Loading songs…").

## The running order
Drag a song up or down in **Running order** to change the playlist's order: the set is planned again in that order (Undo brings back the one before).

## Exporting the mix
**Export the mix: WAV** (24-bit, 44.1 kHz) or **MP3 320**: choose where to save it, and GLUE mixes the set into that file, song by song, in the background (a 2-hour set takes a few minutes). **Cancel** stops it. The **tracklist** (each song's time in the mix) can be copied or saved as text, for Mixcloud or SoundCloud.

The set is kept with the playlist: open it again to listen or export again. Changing the playlist's songs plans the set again.
