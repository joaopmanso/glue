## Mixing a playlist as a set
Right-click a playlist › **Mix it as a set…** GLUE lays its songs out as a mix, in the playlist's order: each song comes in on its first downbeat, on the beat of the one playing, on a 16-bar phrase, before its outro (from each song's sound profile).

- **Style:** **One at a time** (an 8-bar blend), **Continuous** (32-bar blends with a bass swap, shorter for short songs), **Double drops** (the next song's first drop lands on one of the playing song's drops, when their tempos are within 6 % and their keys fit; otherwise a bass swap). Songs whose tempos are more than 8 % apart are cut on the phrase instead.
- **Decks:** 2 or 3 lanes; the songs take turns.
- **Tempo:** each song's own (held through a hand-over, eased between), or **One tempo** for the whole set.
- **Key lock:** songs sped up or slowed down keep their key (on by default).
- **Hand-overs:** each one's style can be changed: Blend, Bass swap, Filter sweep, Echo out, Cut, Double drop.
- Songs are levelled to the set's middle loudness. Songs need their analysis (a tempo, and a beat grid from your DJ app, Prepare, or GLUE's own placement, marked "grid guessed").

## Exporting the mix
**Export the mix: WAV** (24-bit, 44.1 kHz) or **MP3 320**: choose where to save it, and GLUE mixes the set into that file, song by song, in the background (a 2-hour set takes a few minutes). **Cancel** stops it. The **tracklist** (each song's time in the mix) can be copied or saved as text, for Mixcloud or SoundCloud.

The set is kept with the playlist: open it again to export it again. Changing the playlist's songs plans the set again.
