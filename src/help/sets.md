## Mixing a playlist as a set
Right-click a playlist › **Mix it as a set…** GLUE lays its songs out as a mix, in the playlist's order, on the beat of the one playing, on a 16-bar phrase, before its outro (from each song's sound profile).

Each song's **drop** is found where its bassline comes in (a kick alone falls away between the beats, a bassline holds the lows up; read from the song's waveform, or from its loudness when there's none). The next song comes in so its drop lands just where the bass changes hands; a song with a short intro comes in from its first downbeat, and the bass changes hands on its drop. So the playing song is never left without its bass while the next one is still in its intro. The first time a playlist is mixed, GLUE makes the songs' waveforms for this ("Finding the drops"); **Plan again** (a style, a length) uses them for a set planned before.

- **Style:** **One at a time** (an 8-bar blend), **Continuous** (32-bar blends with a bass swap, shorter for short songs), **Double drops** (the next song's first drop lands on one of the playing song's drops, when their tempos are within 6 % and their keys fit; otherwise a bass swap). Songs whose tempos are more than 8 % apart are cut on the phrase instead.
- **Decks:** 2 or 3 lanes; the songs take turns. On **3 decks**, two songs in a row that sound alike (with close tempos and keys that fit) are layered: the second comes in early and plays along with the first for 48 bars, and the next one comes in before the first goes, three at once for 16 bars. Only one of them has its bass at a time.
- **Length:** **No limit**, or 30 minutes to 3 hours: every song plays a little shorter to fit (an earlier phrase), and if that isn't enough, the last songs are left out.
- **Tempo:** each song's own (held through a hand-over, eased between), or **One tempo** for the whole set.
- **Key lock:** songs sped up or slowed down keep their key (on by default).
- Songs are levelled to the set's middle loudness, and where two play at full together (a double drop) both come down a little, as you'd ride the faders. Songs need their analysis (a tempo, and a beat grid from your DJ app, Prepare, or GLUE's own placement, marked "grid guessed").

## The timeline
Each song is drawn where it plays, as you'll hear it: its waveform gets smaller as it fades and loses its lows while its bass is cut, over a faint copy of the song as it is. Behind the songs, a faint grid of bars (brighter every 4 bars, in the accent colour every 16-bar phrase) helps line things up.

The play button and the timeline stay at the top of the window while you scroll down the running order.

- **Mouse wheel:** zoom in and out (around the pointer), out as far as the whole set; **Fit** shows the whole set.
- **Drag** empty space, or a song that isn't selected, to move along the set.
- **Click a song** to select it: its round menu opens where you clicked, with everything about it in one place: **play** from it, its level **+1 / −1 dB**, **key lock**, come in **earlier** or **later**, **Mix in** and **Mix out** (the hand-over from the song before, or into the next, opens beside the menu), let go of its bass edits, and its page. Hover a button to read what it does. Then **drag the song** to bring it in earlier or later (the songs after it move with it, and the one before plays on as it was), or **drag its edges** to start or end it later or earlier. Click it again, or Escape, to close the menu.
- **Press and hold** on any song (without moving) to grab it, then drag it.
- **Shift or Ctrl + click** adds songs to the selection (or takes one out). Dragging any of them moves them all together, and the songs not selected stay where they are; their box sets their level and key lock, or brings them in earlier or later.
- **The bass over part of a song:** hover a song and a **bass** strip shows along its foot, growing as the pointer reaches it. Drag along it to cut the song's bass over those bars, or to open it where it was cut (in a hand-over, say). Stretches cut are hatched red, stretches opened orange; each has a small **×** to let it go.
- Everything moves a bar at a time, and only as far as the set still holds together: no silence between songs, no song cut short of a bar.
- **Undo** and **Redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) step back through every change. Escape closes a box.

## Hand-overs
Each hand-over has a chip under the lanes (and a line in the running order). Click it and its box opens right there: how it hands over (**Blend, Bass swap, Layer, Filter sweep, Echo out, Cut, Double drop**), whose **bass** plays (**Auto** as the style has it, **Outgoing**, **Incoming**, or **Both** for a double bass), 4 bars longer or shorter, the next song in earlier or later, how alike the two songs sound, and **Hear it** (it plays from 8 bars before). Where the bass changes hands (as planned, on the next song's drop), an orange **bass** marker shows on the timeline: drag it to move the swap, a bar at a time. A bass swap turns on it: the next song fades in up to it, and the one playing fades out after it.

## Listening
**Play** (or Space) plays the mix from the playhead, through your chosen output. Click or drag in the **time bar** on top to move the playhead, or click a song's time in the running order to play from there. The preview is the same mixer as the exported file, so what you hear is what you get. Songs are loaded as the mix reaches them ("Loading songs…").

With **GLUE Home** running on this computer (0.84 or later), it does the mixing: it reads the songs from your disk, mixes them, and streams the mix to the page ("Mixed by GLUE Home"), for listening and for the exported file. Nothing is copied into the browser. If a song isn't on that computer, the page says so and mixes in the browser instead.

## The running order
Drag a song up or down in **Running order** to change the playlist's order: the set is planned again in that order (Undo brings back the one before). The song playing fills its line as it goes, and the hand-over playing lights up. A song's name there opens its menu on the timeline, which stays in view above.

A playlist mixed as a set shows a crossfader mark beside its name in the sidebar: click it to open the set.

## Exporting the mix
**Export the mix: WAV** (24-bit, 44.1 kHz) or **MP3 320**: choose where to save it, and GLUE mixes the set into that file, song by song, in the background (a 2-hour set takes a few minutes). **Cancel** stops it.

To export only **a part** of the set, choose **A part**, then the first and last song: they're marked on the time bar, and the first of them comes in alone, from where it starts in the set.

**The tracklist, with times** opens the tracklist in its own window, to copy or save as text, for Mixcloud or SoundCloud.

The set is kept with the playlist: open it again to listen or export again. Changing the playlist's songs plans the set again.
